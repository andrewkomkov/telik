Privacy policy

Last change: 3 November 2026.

Short version

Telik collects no personal data. It has no analytics, no ads and no account. Telik sends nothing to its developer or to any other server of its own.

What stays on the TV

  • Settings, the order of the apps on the Home screen, the favorites and the list of apps that you opened from Telik.
  • The keys that pair the TV with your iPhone, Apple Watch and Apple Home.
  • A short technical log of connections and errors. You can see it in the Telik settings under Diagnostics.

Telik does not send this data off the TV. When you uninstall Telik, Android deletes it.

Network use

  • AirPlay, the remote and Apple Home work only in your local network, between your devices.
  • When an iPhone app sends a video over AirPlay, for example YouTube, the TV loads that video from the address that the app gives. That server belongs to the app, not to Telik.

Permissions

  • Accessibility service: it presses the TV buttons for the iPhone remote and puts the text from the iPhone keyboard into the focused text field. Telik does not save or send screen content.
  • Notification access (optional): Telik uses it only to show which media plays now and to pause or skip it. Telik does not read or store notifications.
  • Local network and Bluetooth: for AirPlay, the remote and headphones.

Contact

Questions go to the issues of the project on GitHub.

Telik is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple or Google.
