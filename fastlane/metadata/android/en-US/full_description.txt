Telik runs on your Android TV and works with the apps already on your iPhone.

* Apple TV Remote in iPhone Control Center: touch area, click, back, play and pause
* iPhone keyboard for standard Android text fields, including Cyrillic
* AirPlay screen mirroring and audio from iPhone, iPad and Mac
* A TV tile in Apple Home with power, volume and app inputs

Protected video such as Apple TV+ and Netflix is not available over AirPlay. Telik does not decrypt DRM.

Accessibility access is required to press remote buttons and to receive keyboard text. Telik has no analytics and no account.
